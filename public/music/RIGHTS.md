## Scope

Audio and supplied/generated OFFSCREEN image assets are excluded from the code's
MIT license. No additional standalone redistribution license is granted by this
repository. Use your own licensed audio and images in forks.

Analysis JSON is a machine-generated list of measured attacks and energy; it is
not a transcription of any other game's charts. Pad, opening and result sound
effects are original Web Audio synthesis, with no third-party game sound assets.
